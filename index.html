Estructuras Clave A1

¿Cómo usar el Verbo "To Be" con Adjetivos?

1. Afirmativo (+)

Sujeto + Verb To Be

She is tired.

They are cold.

2. Negativo (-)

Sujeto + Verb + NOT

She is not (isn't) tired.

They are not (aren't) cold.

3. Pregunta (?)

Verb To Be + Sujeto...?

Is she tired?

Are they cold?
